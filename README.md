## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by first understanding what the user is trying to accomplish and then breaking those needs down into specific features or requirements. User stories are helpful because they describe a feature from the user's point of view. They help developers understand who needs the feature, what they need, and why it is important. Creating user stories also makes larger requirements easier to organize into smaller tasks that can be developed and tested throughout a project.

## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

When developing programs, I think it is important to plan the requirements first and then break the work into smaller and manageable tasks. I also believe that software should be tested throughout development instead of waiting until everything is completed. In my future development work, I would like to incorporate Agile practices such as sprint planning, daily Scrum meetings, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and maintaining a prioritized product backlog. These practices allow a team to communicate regularly, respond to changes, and continuously improve the product.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly, being dependable, respecting other team members, and being willing to collaborate. Software projects often involve people with different responsibilities, so everyone needs to understand how their work contributes to the overall project. A good team member should also be open to feedback, communicate problems early, and help the team reach its shared goals rather than focusing only on individual tasks.
